Our Proven Roof Replacement Process

Six simple steps to a beautiful, long-lasting roof you'll love.

1

First Contact

Call us or fill out the contact form and we will talk to you about the roof issues you're having, and schedule your inspection. We'll also send you over a buyer's guide that explains everything that we do for your new roof.

2

Consultation and Inspection

Your project manager will speak to you and get all the details about your roofing system. During your roof inspection, your project manager will identify any areas where there is damage, take photos of the damage, and provide you with an inspection report.

3

Estimate

After your inspection, we'll review a clear, detailed estimate with you. Every component, materials, ventilation, flashing, and warranties is explained so you understand exactly what's included and why it matters. No vague line items. No surprises. Just clarity so you can make a confident decision.

4

Installation

On installation day, your dedicated superintendent manages every detail from start to finish. Our crew follows manufacturer specifications and building code requirements to ensure your roof is installed the right way, not the fast way. Throughout the project, you'll receive video progress updates and have a clear point of contact for any questions.

5

Clean Up

We protect your home before we start and keep the job site organized throughout the project. After installation, we perform a full cleanup and magnetic sweep to remove nails and debris. We don't consider the job done until your property is clean and safe.

6

Ongoing Support

When your roof is complete, you'll receive warranty documentation and project details for your records. If you ever have questions, we're here. With a true in-house warranty, you have long-term support you can count on.
